        How ordering works

        You won't pay at a checkout. You send us your order, we confirm what's ready and invoice you, then we deliver it.

        1. Fill your order

          Tick a pot size on any tag and add it. Delivery starts at a $350 order, and your order sheet shows how close you are.

        2. Send the request

          Add your name, suburb and phone, then send it by WhatsApp or SMS straight to the nursery.

        3. We call to confirm

          We confirm stock and your delivery day, then send an invoice. Pay by credit card, PayPal or Alipay.

        4. Delivered to you

          Deliveries run Mon–Sat, 1–9 pm, across Perth Metro and Mandurah, usually within 7–10 business days.

        Good to know

        Do you sell gift cards?

        Yes. Gift cards can be used for any purchase. Call or message the nursery to arrange one.

        Will my plant come with care instructions?

        Yes, every plant comes with its own care instructions. Most tags here also show the light it likes.

        My plant isn't looking healthy. What should I do?

        Check its sunlight, watering and soil first. If it's still struggling, call 0420 493 944 and we'll help you sort it out.

        Can I pick up from the nursery?

        We take orders online only and deliver them. Call the office (Mon–Sat, 9 am – 12 noon) if you have a special request.

        From a hobby to a nursery

        Pot Plant Heaven started as a therapeutic hobby for Stephen Rolls, a way to find solace and recover from illness. It grew into a way to help others: the best plants, prices and delivery costs he can offer across Perth, supplied from south of the river.

        “All plants are suitable for the Perth environment and will be guaranteed to provide you with the tools to create a majestic garden that others will be jealous of, asking ‘Where did you get that from?’”

        Stephen Rolls, Pot Plant Heaven Perth
