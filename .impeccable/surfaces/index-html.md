# Surface brief: storefront (index.html)

Scope: single-page storefront covering the hero, the catalogue ("the bed"), the plant detail view, the order sheet, how ordering works, delivery and hours, Stephen's story, and the footer. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The catalogue and order sheet behave like Operate, but they live inside that world.

Audience and job: Perth gardeners on a phone in daylight, plus bulk hedging buyers. They come to find a plant that suits their spot, pick a pot size, reach the $350 minimum, and send an order request. Proof: 640 real lines with real photos, pot sizes and prices; Stephen's own words; hours and delivery facts. Constraints: no popups, no bots, no floating widgets over the plants, no invented reviews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every plant is its own nursery stake label: the tag is the product card, the detail page and the cart line. This refuses the airy white grid with sage product cards and a lifestyle hero.

OWN-WORLD: White injection-moulded tag plastic with a punched hole, rounded shoulders and a pointed stake, set on a saturated nursery-green field (#0d6b38 family). The tags carry black condensed caps for common names, italic botanical names, and drawn light glyphs (sun, part, shade). There is a process-yellow price flash, signal red only for out-of-stock or warnings, and black poly-pot plastic for the deepest ground (footer, 3D pot). Archivo variable throughout: wdth 62 / 850 for names, italic for botanicals.

STORY: Visitors see this is a real Perth nursery with hundreds of plants (not one), that prices and pot sizes are upfront, and that delivery has a $350 minimum. They fill an order on tags and send it by WhatsApp or SMS, and Stephen confirms.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The green field is full-bleed. Above it sit a black utility strip (online orders only · Perth Metro & Mandurah · Mon–Sat 1–9pm · $350 min · 0420 493 944) and a header (tag wordmark, nav, Order button with count and total). Centre stage is a WebGL black nursery pot with a procedural plant and a giant stake tag pushed into it. The tag's face shows a real featured plant (photo, name, pot, price). Behind it, the H1 is set in huge white condensed caps across the full width and partly occluded by the pot. Directly under the pot sits the working action: prev/next tag controls and "Add <plant> <pot> — $X", then the catalogue search set as a blank tag. Signature interaction: drag to spin the pot, which turns the tag to its back (care line and sizes); next/prev pulls the tag out and pushes the next plant's tag in. Motion grammar: tags lift and tilt like being pulled from a bed, a springy settle with ease-out and no bounce loops; reduced-motion shows the static tag.

FORM: Nursery Tag, position 1 on the ordered list (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the rolled Delivery Docket); seed key feb48903.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
